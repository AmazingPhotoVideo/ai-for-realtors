# Realtor creative platform

The Realtor creative platform turns verified listing and brand information into reusable public marketing assets. It starts with three connected toolkits:

1. **Design packs** — print flyers, brochure layouts, square posts, portrait posts, and stories.
2. **Single-property pages** — three static, mobile-first property-site designs for manual listing data.
3. **Agent website + CRM builder** — three Realtor website directions and a lightweight lead-management starter.

Each toolkit includes fictional sample data, reusable templates, a population workflow, validation, and a Claude/Hermes-compatible skill for nontechnical setup.

## Choose a starting point

| Need | Toolkit | Good first output |
|---|---|---|
| Launch a listing across print and social | [`design-packs/`](../design-packs/) | Letter flyer + square post + story |
| Publish a dedicated page for one listing | [`property-pages/`](../property-pages/) | Mobile property page with gallery and showing CTA |
| Build an agent brand site and lead pipeline | [`agent-sites/`](../agent-sites/) | Branded site preset + manual featured listings + CRM starter |

## Shared workflow

1. Collect the agent's approved brand assets, brokerage requirements, and jurisdiction.
2. Collect listing facts and media from an authorized source.
3. Mark unknown facts as unknown; never fill gaps with plausible copy.
4. Select a design direction based on the brand and audience—not personal demographics or protected classes.
5. Generate into a new output folder rather than editing master templates.
6. Review copy, crop, overflow, contrast, links, contact details, forms, consent, disclaimers, and attribution.
7. Obtain seller/brokerage/board approval where required before publishing.

## APV services and integrations

These templates are designed for self-service use. Realtors who want professional property photography, video, drone, 3D tours, floor plans, virtual staging, or a done-with-you implementation can contact [Amazing Photo Video](https://amazingphotovideo.com).

Manual featured listings and manually entered single-property facts work without an MLS feed. Live MLS, VOW, IDX, sold-data, or board integrations are not bundled. They require the appropriate board, brokerage, vendor, data, and display permissions. Contact APV to discuss the project before connecting a live feed.

## Attribution and commercial use

Public outputs retain an APV credit and backlink by default. Attribution-free, white-label, source-resale, and higher-volume commercial use require a written agreement. Read the [APV design asset licensing policy](./design-asset-licensing.md) before client or resale use.

The licensing policy describes the intended attribution-based reseller allowance and revenue-share threshold. Legal review is recommended before APV relies on those terms as a final commercial licence.

## Important boundaries

- Templates do not grant rights to listing data, photos, video, maps, fonts, logos, testimonials, or third-party feeds.
- Never imply APV created media it did not create.
- Do not invent prices, measurements, room counts, rankings, testimonials, sales volume, designations, neighbourhood claims, or market statistics.
- Do not describe areas using protected-class proxies or steering language.
- Generated sites are not brokerage, legal, privacy, accessibility, CASL/TCPA, MLS, VOW, or IDX approval.
- Human review remains required before export, deployment, or publication.
