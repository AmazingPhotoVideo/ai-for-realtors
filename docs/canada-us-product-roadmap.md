# Canada–United States Realtor product roadmap

This roadmap turns the repository audit into practical products for working real-estate professionals. It prioritizes useful, source-grounded tools that can create ethical awareness of [Amazing Photo Video](https://amazingphotovideo.com) without implying APV photographed, approved, or endorsed a listing when it did not.

> **Scope:** Product planning, not legal advice. Federal, provincial/state, local board/MLS, brokerage, platform, and property-specific rules must remain separate. Every compliance-sensitive product should display source URLs and a `last_verified` date.

## Recommended build sequence

### Wave 1 — fast value and genuine APV fit

1. **Seller Media Readiness Planner** — room-by-room, season, property-type, occupant/privacy, access, weather, drone, and shot-priority planning.
2. **Property Media Specification Calculator** — generates channel-specific photo, video, social, floor-plan, caption, rights, and disclosure requirements.
3. **Truthful Media and AI Edit Ledger** — records originals, edits, approvals, rights, disclosure labels, channels, and checksums.
4. **Seller Marketing Report Builder** — turns manual CSV/JSON activity exports into an accessible weekly HTML/PDF report without treating activity as a sale prediction.

### Wave 2 — trust and compliance infrastructure

5. **Canadian Listing Compliance Passport** and **US Listing Launch Rules Compiler** sharing one versioned jurisdiction schema.
6. **Accessible Listing Marketing Pack and Property Media Auditor** for alt text, captions, transcripts, keyboard use, contrast, forms, and PDF review.
7. **Consent-Safe Open House Kit** with separate operational registration, property-response, and marketing permissions.
8. **Listing Description Verifier and French-Canadian Transcreator** with claim-level provenance and human review.

### Wave 3 — deeper client and brokerage systems

9. Homebuyer cost/program explainers, beginning with Ontario/Toronto and British Columbia.
10. Province/state-aware relocation packs.
11. Pre-market/delayed-marketing decision records.
12. Regulator, association, board/MLS, and source-freshness monitoring.

## Shared data architecture

The highest-priority products should reuse these versioned records:

```text
jurisdiction
  country
  province_or_state
  municipality
  regulator
  association
  board_or_mls
  brokerage_policy
  source_url
  authority_type
  effective_date
  last_verified
  reviewer

media_asset
  asset_id
  property_id
  creator
  copyright_owner
  licence_scope
  seller_or_property_consent
  original_checksum
  derivative_checksum
  edit_categories
  ai_or_virtual_staging_status
  required_disclosure
  approved_channels
  expiry_or_takedown_date
  approval_record
```

Unknown fields must remain `unknown` or block publication. They must never be filled by inference.

## Ethical APV growth rules

- Create links through genuinely useful research, calculators, schemas, examples, and case studies—not keyword-rich footer links intended to manipulate ranking.
- A required public credit must identify the actual APV toolkit used. It must never imply APV photographed, filmed, staged, measured, listed, approved, or endorsed a property unless independently true.
- Keep promotion out of MLS remarks, private client communications, consent language, legal forms, and mandatory disclosures.
- Describe APV accurately as Toronto/GTA-based. United States growth should focus on resources, licensing, remote implementation, and verified partners unless APV actually establishes local field coverage.
- Required APV attribution links are a condition of using and deploying covered APV work. Keep them as normal followed links using the approved APV URL and attribution text; do not add `nofollow` or `sponsored` attributes.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, rankings, performance claims, conversion figures, reviews, or market outcomes.
- Accessibility and compliance products are audits or workflows—not certifications or legal opinions.
