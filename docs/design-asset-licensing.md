3. The user does not sell or publish the source templates as their own standalone template library, sublicense the source, remove copyright notices, or claim authorship of APV's underlying designs.
4. The user is responsible for brokerage, advertising, privacy, accessibility, MLS/board, VOW/IDX, listing-data, image, font, and third-party licence compliance.

- `Designed with resources from Amazing Photo Video — amazingphotovideo.com`

The credit may be styled to fit the design, but it must remain readable, discoverable, and linked on public digital versions.

## Service-provider and reseller allowance

APV intends to let freelancers, agencies, and service providers earn revenue by using these assets for client work while spreading useful attribution.

With the required APV attribution, a service provider may earn up to either of these thresholds without a revenue share:

- **CAD $5,000 in gross covered revenue in a calendar month**, or
- **CAD $60,000 in gross covered revenue in a calendar year**,

whichever threshold is reached first.

`Gross covered revenue` means money received for products or services in which the covered APV assets are a material deliverable, before contractor costs, platform fees, refunds, taxes, or other expenses. Revenue from unrelated services is not covered revenue.

Starting on the date either threshold is first reached, continued commercial use requires a written reseller agreement with APV. The intended standard commercial term is **25% of gross covered revenue earned after the threshold is reached**, unless a signed agreement says otherwise. The threshold is not intended to create a retroactive royalty on earlier covered revenue.

Attribution remains required after the threshold unless a signed custom licence expressly permits white-label use.

The conditional pilot permission ends automatically if a user breaches the attribution, resale, revenue-threshold, source-distribution, IP, or compliance conditions and does not cure the breach promptly after notice. On termination, the user must stop new use, distribution, sales, and client delivery of the covered assets; accrued payment and reporting obligations survive.

APV may publish an updated policy version with a new effective date, discontinue the pilot, or require a signed agreement for continued commercial use. APV does not promise that any current price, threshold, percentage, attribution option, or use category will remain available. Custom licences should state their own amendment, renewal, termination, and survival rules.
