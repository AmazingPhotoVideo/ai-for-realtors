**Labels:** **Ready** = use now; **Blueprint** = written plan/copy to implement in your own tools; **Developer starter** = working foundation that needs setup, verified data, and testing.

## Public resources, private client content, and APV credits

Prompts, tutorials, and other public learning resources can be browsed and reused as described in their files. Covered design, page, site, and other creative assets follow APV's separate [design asset pilot policy](./docs/design-asset-licensing.md). The repository as a whole is not presented as open source or entirely free.

Keep client names, contact details, access instructions, financial information, and confidential notes out of public examples. Do not add promotional credits to private intake, CRM notes, consent receipts, or one-to-one follow-up. Public outputs that use covered APV creative assets must retain the credit required by their policy and skill instructions. Learn more at [Amazing Photo Video](https://amazingphotovideo.com).
